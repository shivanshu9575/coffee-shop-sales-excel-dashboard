# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis (Excel Dashboard)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data for a coffee shop chain to uncover actionable insights that can improve business performance. The analysis covers **149,116 transactions** across **3 store locations** (Astoria, Hell's Kitchen, Lower Manhattan) using data cleaning, pivot tables, and an interactive dashboard built entirely in **Microsoft Excel**.

## 🛠 Tools & Techniques Used
- **Microsoft Excel**
  - Data cleaning & preprocessing (raw transaction-level data, 18 columns)
  - Helper columns (Month Name, Day Name, Hour, Day of Week, Total Bill, etc.)
  - **PivotTables** for aggregating sales, footfall, and product-level metrics
  - **PivotCharts** (line chart, pie charts, bar charts) for visual analysis
  - **Slicers** (Month Name, Day Name) for interactive filtering
  - KPI cards for Total Sales, Total Footfall, Avg Bill/Person, Avg Order/Person

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights
- **Total Sales:** $6,98,812.33
- **Total Footfall:** 149,116
- **Avg Bill/Person:** 4.69
- **Avg Order/Person:** 1.44
- Quantity ordered trend by hour of day (peak hours identified)
- Sales distribution by product category (Coffee, Tea, Bakery, etc.)
- Order size distribution (Large / Regular / Small)
- Footfall & sales comparison across store locations
- Top 5 best-selling products by sales
- Order trends across weekdays

## 🔎 Business Questions Answered
- How do sales vary by day of the week and hour of the day? Are there peak times?
- What is the total sales revenue for each month?
- How do sales vary across different store locations?
- What is the average bill/order value per person?
- Which products are the best-sellers by quantity and revenue?
- How do sales vary by product category and type?

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Download `coffee_shop_sales_dashboard.xlsx` and open it in Excel.
2. Go to the **Dashboard** sheet.
3. Use the **Month Name** and **Day Name** slicers on the left to filter the entire dashboard interactively.
4. `coffee_shop_sales_data.xlsx` has the raw, un-pivoted transaction data used to build the dashboard.

## 📈 Key Takeaways
- Sales peak between **8 AM–10 AM**, aligning with morning coffee rush.
- **Coffee (39%)** and **Tea (28%)** together drive the majority of category-wise sales.
- **Barista Espresso** is the top-selling product, generating over **$91K** in sales.
- Sales are fairly consistent across all three store locations, with Hell's Kitchen slightly ahead.
- Weekday order volume is stable, with no major weekend dip — indicating steady daily demand.
